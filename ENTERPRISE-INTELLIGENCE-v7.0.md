# RP IA Enterprise Intelligence Platform v7.0

This release adds operational foundations for:

- Natural Language Engine with employee, advancement, knowledge, action, qualification, shift, and Critical Gate intents.
- Competency Coach Engine with positive development guidance and next-step recommendations.
- Experience Engine that summarizes recurring challenge patterns from assessment history.
- Evidence Engine data model and audit integration.
- Certification Engine data model, expiration status, and upcoming-expiration calculation.
- Independent Knowledge Center with search, article creation, editing, approval status, version, review date, and full article display.
- RP Brain integration with approved Knowledge Center articles.
- Plant Intelligence Dashboard combining readiness, actions, Critical Gates, certifications, and experience patterns.
- Clickable dashboard KPIs for active associates and corrective actions.

## Important scope note

This is an advanced local pilot foundation. Natural language understanding uses deterministic local intent and entity logic rather than a large language model. Enterprise-wide synchronization, secure server-side authorization, shared files, and centrally protected audit records still require the backend/API and company infrastructure described in the enterprise specification.
